Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SANIP, EQASA certification, and our programmes.

Registration & Enrollment

How do I register for SANIP?

You can register online through our Shop page. Simply select the module(s) you wish to pursue, add them to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay online using our secure payment system.

Do I need to complete all modules in order?

While modules are designed to build progressively, you may start with any module. However, we recommend beginning with Module 1 if you're new to equestrian instruction. Some modules do require prerequisites.

What if I already have experience? Can I skip modules?

Yes! SANIP offers Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL). You can apply to test out of modules where you have equivalent knowledge and experience. See our RPL page for details.

How long is each module?

Module duration varies. Theory-based modules typically take 3-6 months of self-paced study, while practical modules may take longer depending on your training pace. You have up to 2 years to complete any module.

Exams & Assessment

When are exams held?

EQASA exams are held at multiple locations throughout South Africa, typically 2-4 times per year. Check our Exam Dates page for provincial exam schedules and registration deadlines.

What is the practical assessment like?

Practical assessments include riding demonstrations and teaching observations. Assessors evaluate your horsemanship, teaching ability, communication, and horse welfare practices according to EQASA standards.

What if I don't pass an exam?

Don't worry - you can retake the exam at the next available sitting. We provide detailed feedback to help you prepare better. Most candidates pass on their second attempt with additional study and practice.

Is the certificate valid internationally?

EQASA certification is recognized throughout South Africa and has strong standing in the Southern African region. International recognition depends on your destination country's requirements. Contact us for specifics about your situation.

Fees & Payment

How much does SANIP cost?

Fees are charged per module. Visit our Fees page to see current pricing for each module. Discounts are available for multiple module registrations and group registrations.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards, debit cards, and EFT transfers. Our online payment system is secure and encrypted. You'll receive an immediate confirmation email after payment.

Is there a payment plan available?

Yes. For modules over R2000, we offer a 2-installment payment option. Contact us for details about payment arrangements.

Certificates & Qualifications

What certificate do I receive after completing a module?

You receive an EQASA certificate for each module successfully completed, including your certificate level (1-4). The certificate shows your achievements and is recognized throughout South Africa.

How long does a certificate remain valid?

EQASA certificates don't expire. However, we encourage instructors to pursue continuous professional development and additional modules to stay current with industry standards.

Can I upgrade my certificate?

Yes. After completing modules at one level, you can pursue modules at the next level to upgrade your qualification. This provides structured progression from Basic through Master Instructor levels.

Still have questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our team is here to help!
