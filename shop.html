EQASA Registration

Through its membership of the International Group for Equestrian Qualifications, EQASA qualification holders at Levels 1–3 can apply for International Equestrian Passports. EQASA also offers specialist Instructor/Coach certifications, subject to the applicable syllabus requirements.

All candidates are required to register on the SANIP website for the prescribed fee.

Examination Bookings

Full examinations and supplementary examinations are booked through the individual EQASA examination events. Available tickets, prices and booking conditions are shown on each event.

Important Information

EQASA is an independent external assessment body and not a training provider. Candidates are responsible for self-study and practical preparation. EQASA does not offer preparedness training for examinations and does not endorse or recommend a particular training establishment.
