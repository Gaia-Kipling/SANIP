EQASA

NPC 2013/065969/08

Formerly the SANEF National Instructors’ Plan.

EQASA is a member of the International Group for Equestrian Qualifications (IGEQ), a member of the Council for Equine & Equestrian Professionals of South Africa (CEEPSA), and an Assessment Quality Partner with the Quality Council for Trades & Occupations (QCTO).

About Us

“EDUCATION BEFORE PARTICIPATION”

Since 1976, equestrian education in southern Africa has achieved substantial growth across many learning pathways through certification, formerly under the banner of the SANEF National Instructors’ Plan. Equine and equestrian industry needs and the changing educational environment of the world in general and South Africa in particular determined that the scope and reach of our programmes required a transformation of the NIP into EQASA.

EQASA’s vision is: To address all equine and equestrian educational needs for the benefit of all and to grow pride in certified knowledge.

In addition to a variety of stand-alone subject skills competency certifications, 12 levels of certification are offered.

FAQ - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EXAM BOOKINGS

Only candidates who are registered with EQASA can book an examination. Please login, then select “Book Examinations” on the Examinations drop down. The last date for bookings and payments is shown against the selected date and venue as “Allowed registration date”. Please ensure that your on-line bookings and exam payments are made by the published cut-off dates. Please make sure that we have your up-to-date email address.

Candidates ages 16 and 17 are reminded that written consent from a parent or legal guardian is required to register on the website and to take an examination.

ALUMNI LISTS

The Alumni lists have also been amended. Please check to see that you are listed – if not please contact us. Please check that we have your correct surname.

CEEPSA REGISTERED INSTRUCTORS

Candidates are required to complete two riding proficiency tests prior to module four; to be signed off by a CEEPSA registered instructor. Please contact CEEPSA to have your name added to the provincial lists that can be given to candidates who need these tests to be signed off.

CANDIDATES PLEASE NOTE

All candidates are reminded to please ensure that they are familiar with our syllabus; assessment requirements and conditions of application. Once registered on the website, you have agreed to the conditions of application.

On achieving a pass of a module, please check your details on the Alumni Lists.

A pass of any EQASA module entitles you to join the professional body CEEPSA. Members of CEEPSA will obtain a professional designation as either an Equine Practitioner, or an Equestrian Practitioner. CEEPSA is recognised by SAQA and its designations are registered with SAQA.
