Examination Regulations & Conditions of Application

2.1 Registration Requirements

  1. All candidates must register their details on the website. The registration fee provides access to EQASA services.
  2. Candidates must accept the terms and conditions through EQASA registration and must know the syllabus requirements for the module being booked.
  3. An applicant may not take an assessment if found guilty by a court of law of contravening South African horse-welfare legislation.

2.2 Examination Booking Requirements

  1. Candidates may apply at any EQASA regional assessment centre or host venue.
  2. Bookings are made through the website and paid at booking via PayFast.
  3. Modules must be completed fully and in sequence.
  4. The individual candidate must book by the advertised closing date. A booking does not necessarily guarantee inclusion in the next assessment; places are first-come, first-served once payment is received.
  5. Assessment dates appear in the exam-booking section of the website.
  6. Candidates must stay in contact with the relevant regional director/exam coordinator by email regarding bookings, dates, venues and times.
  7. Special-needs candidates should notify the regional director by email when booking so appropriate assessment arrangements can be considered.
  8. Because assessments are practical, some disabilities may prevent a candidate from completing a particular assessment; EQASA may advise on alternative qualifications or training opportunities.
  9. Assessments are conducted in English. With three months' written notice, EQASA will endeavour to obtain a translator for Modules 1–3 only.
  10. Module 5 candidates must have Grade 12 or an equivalent school-leaving certificate.
  11. Candidates should use the document-upload facility on their profile.
Important: The published date is the start date of an exam. In Gauteng and Western Cape, high application numbers can cause an exam to run over several days; the published date then represents the start of the exam week.

2.3 Candidate Cancellation Policy

  1. Assessment fees are non-refundable, but on written application by email they may be held over for another date within 12 months of payment. After 12 months, applicable price increases may apply.
  2. Candidates are responsible for re-booking. After making a new booking, EQASA must be notified by email so the previous payment can be carried over.
  3. For cancellation less than 30 days before a confirmed assessment, 25% of the assessment fee is forfeited for administration.
  4. A no-show or cancellation less than 7 days before a confirmed assessment forfeits the relevant examination fees.
  5. For a medical reason for late cancellation, a doctor's certificate is required; for Module 5 riding, a veterinary certificate is required to postpone without charge.

2.4 Results & Certificates

  1. Results are posted approximately four weeks after examination documents reach the results moderator. This may be about one week after the final exam day. Candidates access results using their login details; results are not given by telephone or to third parties.
  2. Certificates can be couriered on request for a R180 door-to-door courier fee.
  3. Otherwise certificates are sent to the regional director for collection by the candidate.

2.5 Supplementary Examinations

  1. Supplementary assessments may be taken after at least one month for Modules 1–2 or two months for Module 3 from the original assessment.
  2. For practical subjects at Module 4 and higher, at least six months must elapse before a rewrite.
  3. Supplementary or stand-alone subjects must be booked and paid for one month before the published examination date.
  4. All supplementary examinations must be completed within two years of the original examination.
  5. Supplementary fees are listed in the fees document.
  6. Supplementary subjects must be booked as a Supplementary Exam on the website.

2.6 Examination Day

  1. Candidates must be physically competent and wear correct safety and riding wear for practical assessments.
  2. Cell-phone use is prohibited during assessments and phones are not allowed in written tests except for emergencies as permitted by the rules.
  3. Jewellery should be restricted to a wristwatch; body-piercing loops, rings or studs must be removed or concealed for safety.
  4. Candidates may not take an assessment while pregnant or with a temporary physical impairment such as broken bones.
  5. Candidates must comply with the specific requirements for each module in the syllabus.
  6. Assessments may be cancelled, altered or postponed when circumstances require it; EQASA cannot refund travel or accommodation expenses in these circumstances.
  7. Candidates may appeal if an assessment was not conducted according to syllabus requirements; the appeal form is available from the national moderator.
  8. After a rider fall, the examiner may prevent continuation of that component; a rewrite of the subject may be permitted at the next assessment date.
  9. Show-jumping rules apply to jumping sections from Module 5 upward.
  10. Candidates must work safely during practical assessments, including using appropriate techniques when securing horses.
  11. All examinations use set assessment standards. Venue amenities may vary between provinces, and candidates are responsible for knowing the syllabus and conditions of application.

Competition-discipline assessments not specified in the syllabus are available on request from the Managing Director.
