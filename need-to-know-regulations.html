EQASA Regulations & Standards

Professional standards, code of conduct, and regulations for all EQASA certified instructors.

Code of Conduct

All EQASA certified professionals commit to the following principles:

  • Safety First: Prioritize the safety and welfare of horses and riders in all activities
  • Professional Excellence: Maintain high standards of teaching and training
  • Continuous Learning: Stay current with industry developments and best practices
  • Ethical Practice: Conduct business with integrity and honesty
  • Horse Welfare: Ensure horses are treated with compassion and respect

Professional Standards

Safety Equipment

All participants must wear approved safety helmets and appropriate riding attire. Trainers must maintain equipment in good condition.

Liability & Insurance

All instructors must maintain professional liability insurance. Current documentation must be kept and proof provided upon request.

Record Keeping

Maintain accurate records of student progress, assessments, and any incidents. Records should be kept for minimum 5 years.

Continuing Professional Development

EQASA certified professionals are required to undertake continuing education. Minimum 20 hours per year is recommended.

Horse Welfare Standards

EQASA certification holders are committed to ensuring the highest standards of horse welfare:

  • Regular veterinary check-ups and health monitoring
  • Appropriate nutrition and living conditions
  • Proper training methods without harsh equipment
  • Adequate rest and recovery time
  • Compassionate handling and care
  • Reporting of any signs of abuse or neglect

Compliance & Discipline

EQASA maintains a rigorous complaints and discipline procedure to ensure all members uphold professional standards. Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in:

  • Written warning
  • Temporary suspension of certification
  • Revocation of certification
  • Legal action in cases of serious violations
