Module 2: Horse Care and Management

Comprehensive training in horse health, nutrition, farrier care, and stable management.

Module Overview

Module 2 focuses on the care and management of horses, covering topics essential for anyone working with equines. Students learn about horse health, nutrition, conformation, farrier care, and stable management practices. This module can be taken after Module 1 or concurrently, typically taking 4-6 weeks.

Learning Objectives

By completing this module, you will be able to:

  • Understand equine anatomy and conformation
  • Assess horse health and recognize common diseases
  • Develop balanced nutrition and feeding programs
  • Understand farrier care and hoof health
  • Manage stable facilities and daily care routines
  • Apply welfare principles in horse management

Course Content

Section 1: Equine Anatomy & Health

  • Skeletal and muscular systems
  • Conformation assessment
  • Common health conditions and symptoms

Section 2: Nutrition & Feeding

  • Nutritional requirements by age and activity
  • Feed types and composition
  • Feeding management and digestive health

Section 3: Farrier Care

  • Hoof anatomy and health
  • Farrier relationships and communication
  • Common hoof problems

Section 4: Stable Management

  • Facility requirements and safety
  • Daily care routines
  • Waste management and hygiene

Assessment & Exams

Theory Exam

Format: 2-hour written exam

Content: All learning objectives and course content

Pass Mark: 60%

Practical Assessment

Format: Practical demonstration of horse care skills

Content: Grooming, tacking, basic health checks, stable management

Pass Mark: 60%
