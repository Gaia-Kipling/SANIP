Module 2 – Stable Yard Skills

SANIP Certificate of Stable Yard Skills – Stage Two

Professional Head Groom

Entrance Requirements

  • The minimum age is the year the candidate turns 17.
  • Candidates under 18 require written permission from their legal guardian.
  • Successful completion of Module 1.

Assessment Information

The minimum pass rate for all subjects is 70%. Candidates are assessed orally on the safe, practical application of their knowledge, with explanations.

Subject Knowledge and Skills

  • 5.2.1 Tack 2 – saddle types and use, saddle fit, broken trees, tack condition, consequences of poor fit and common training aids.
  • 5.2.2 Physiology & Behaviour 2 – safe restraint methods, risks and the importance of observing normal and abnormal behaviour.
  • 5.2.3 Grooming & Turnout – securing and handling horses, improving appearance, mane and tail preparation, hoof products and safe handling of difficult horses.
  • 5.2.4 Travel & Trucking – horse needs during transport, monitoring behaviour and temperature, reducing stress and recognising travel sickness.
  • 5.2.5 Lungeing 1 – safe preparation, equipment, lungeing technique and assessment of the horse's way of going.
  • 5.2.6 Yard Management 2 – practical management of stable-yard routines and responsibilities.
