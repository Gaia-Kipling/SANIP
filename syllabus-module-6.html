Module 6: Professional Practice

Business management, legal responsibility, and professional practice for equestrian instructors.

Module Overview

Module 6 prepares instructors to manage professional practices and equestrian businesses. Topics include business formation, liability insurance, financial management, legal responsibilities, and professional ethics. Essential for independent instructors and facility managers. Duration: 4-6 weeks.

Key Topics

  • Business formation and structure
  • Accounting and financial management
  • Professional liability and insurance
  • Legal responsibilities and contracts
  • Staff management and employment
  • Health and safety regulations
  • Marketing and client relations

Module Details

Duration:4-6 weeks
Study Hours:50-60 hours
Module Fee:R 3,000
Exam Fee:R 500
