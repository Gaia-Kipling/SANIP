Module 6 – International Level 2 Coach

SANIP Certificate – International Level Two Coach

Henry Grimes Syllabus

Entrance Requirements

  • Discipline specialisation is permitted at Level Two.
  • Successful completion of Modules 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5; minimum assessment age is 20 years.
  • At least 75 logged hours of lessons taught in the nominated competition discipline(s), submitted with the application at least one month before assessment.
  • At least 75 logged hours of personal competitive riding in the nominated discipline(s), submitted at least one month before assessment.
  • At least 15 hours worked at a recognised show for each nominated competition discipline; provincial/national judges in the relevant discipline are exempt.
  • Evidence of the candidate’s and pupils’ competition achievements.

Certification Information

  • Minimum pass rate for all subjects: 70%.
  • A recognised Level One First Aid Certificate is required for certification.

Riding Level Two

Candidates ride a known, trained horse in their nominated discipline and an unknown horse on the flat. The assessment expects work appropriate to the discipline. For example, elementary dressage includes correct frame, basic lateral work, counter-canter, simple changes and improving collection; show jumping includes a flagged and numbered course of at least eight fences at 1.1 m, including a combination, with rhythm, style and stride control. The unknown-horse assessment considers the horse’s way of going, schooling level and competition potential.

Communication Skills & Theory of Teaching

A written assessment examines knowledge and application of effective communication and teaching theory.

Practical Coaching

Candidates observe and discuss a rider demonstration with assessors, then teach an individual lesson based on their assessment of the horse-and-rider combination, showing an improvement in performance.

Written Discipline Paper(s)

The discipline paper(s) are written after completion of the practical assessments.
