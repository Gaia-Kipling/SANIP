Examination Credits & Exemptions

EQASA recognises that candidates may have completed competence assessments or formal learning through other organisations.

Pony Club Tests

  • C+ Test: may qualify for exemption from the Riding Proficiency Tests required for Module 4.
  • B Test: may qualify for credits for Modules 1 and 2.
  • AH Test: may qualify for credits for Modules 1 and 2, parts of Module 3 and riding at Module 4.

Applicants must obtain an endorsement letter from the National Chairman of Pony Club confirming the award. The concession applies to Pony Club applicants who obtained their test after 2005.

Equine Studies – Grade 12

Credits may be awarded for Modules 1 and 2, parts of Module 3, riding at Module 4 and the personal riding required at Level One, subject to the published conditions. The applicant must take the jumping option for the riding practical and achieve at least 60% overall for the subject.

Tshwane University of Technology – Equine Science

Applicants who achieved the three-year National Diploma from the TUT Equine Science division since 2000, with at least 60% for the course, may be awarded credits for Module 1, Module 2 and parts of Module 3.

Foreign Qualifications

Through EQASA's membership of the International Group for Equestrian Qualifications, holders of Level 1, 2 or 3 qualifications from IGEQ member countries are recognised in South Africa. Permanent residents of South Africa must hold an International Equestrian Passport issued by EQASA. Candidates should check the IGEQ member-country information to determine whether a foreign qualification is recognised.

Recognition of Prior Learning

People aged 40 or older who have the requisite experience aligned with Modules 1–5 may apply for assessment through RPL if they can submit portfolios of evidence demonstrating at least 20 years of practical and consecutive application of relevant EQASA subjects or composite modules.

If a candidate has a formal assessment from an institution not listed here, they should contact the Managing Director with supporting evidence.
