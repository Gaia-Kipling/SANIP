Examination Credits & Exemptions

Information for candidates who have completed recognised competence assessments elsewhere.

EQASA recognises that candidates may have completed competence assessments with other organisations. Requests for credits or exemptions can be directed to the Managing Director.

Pony Club Tests

  • C+ test: may qualify for an exemption from the Riding Proficiency Tests required for Module 4.
  • B test: may be awarded credits for Modules 1 and 2.
  • AH test: may be awarded credits for Modules 1 and 2 and parts of Module 3 and basic riding at Module 4.

Applicants must obtain a letter of endorsement from the National Chairman of Pony Club confirming the award. The published concession applies to Pony Club applicants who obtained their test after 2005.

Other Prior Learning

Other formal learning or competence assessments may be considered individually. Candidates should contact EQASA with supporting evidence so that the appropriate credit or exemption can be considered.
