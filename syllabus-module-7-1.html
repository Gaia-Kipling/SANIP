Module 7.1: Research & Development

Advanced research methods, training innovation, and contribution to the equestrian profession.

Specialization Overview

This specialization is designed for master-level practitioners who wish to contribute to the advancement of equestrian knowledge through research, publication, and training innovation. Develop practical research skills and create meaningful contributions to the industry.

Topics Covered

  • Research methodology and design
  • Data analysis and interpretation
  • Writing for publication
  • Training program development and innovation
  • Evidence-based practice in equestrian training
  • Mentoring and knowledge transfer

Requirements

Prerequisite: Module 7

Duration: 12-16 weeks

Fee: R 5,000

Note: This specialization includes a significant research project and publication component.
