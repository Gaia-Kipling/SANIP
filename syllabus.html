Syllabus

EQASA's certification pathway progresses through sequential modules from Stable Yard Skills to International Equestrian Coach qualifications.

Certification Pathway

EQASA certificates are ranked stepwise. Progression to the next level is obtained through completion of the required sequential modules. The current EQASA syllabus lists ten syllabus pages covering the core coaching pathway and Western Riding specialisations.

Core Modules

  • Module 1 – Stable Yard Skills — SANIP Certificate of Stable Yard Skills – Stage One / Professional Groom.
  • Module 2 – Stable Yard Skills — SANIP Certificate of Stable Yard Skills – Stage Two / Professional Head Groom.
  • Module 3 – Horsemastership — SANIP Certificate of Horsemastership.
  • Module 4 – Assistant Instructor — SANIP Certificate – Assistant Instructor.
  • Module 5 – International Level 1 Coach — SANIP Certificate – International Level One Coach.
  • Module 6 – International Level 2 Coach — SANIP Certificate – International Level Two Coach.
  • Module 7 – International Level 3 Coach — SANIP Certificate – International Level Three Coach.

Western Riding Specialisations

  • Module 5.1 – International Level 1 Western Riding Coach
  • Module 6.1 – International Level 2 Western Riding Coach
  • Module 7.1 – International Level 3 Western Riding Coach

EQASA also offers specialist Instructor/Coach certification pathways including Western Riding, Equestrian Tourism, Vaulting, Driving, Endurance, Saddle Seat, Polocrosse, Mounted Games, Tentpegging, Polo, Mounted Policing and Therapeutic instruction in association with SARDA. Requirements from Module 4 onward for these specialisations are provided by EQASA on request.

General Requirements

Practical experience is a prerequisite. Candidates are responsible for self-study, and EQASA does not offer preparedness training for examinations.

A recognised Level One First Aid Certificate is compulsory for Modules 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7. Candidates may take examinations before obtaining First Aid certification, but EQASA certification is only awarded once candidates hold a current recognised certificate.

Individual module pages contain the specific entrance requirements, assessment information and subject requirements for each qualification.
