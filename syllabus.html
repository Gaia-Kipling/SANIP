SANIP Syllabus

Comprehensive seven-module curriculum covering all aspects of equestrian instruction and professional development.

Modules Overview

Our syllabus is structured in seven progressive modules, each building upon the previous. Learners may choose to complete individual modules or progress through all seven levels to earn complete certification.

Module 1: Foundations of Horsemanship

Introduction to equestrian principles, horse psychology, basic care and management, and fundamental riding techniques. Essential foundation for all instructors.

Status: Foundational | Duration: Self-paced

Module 2: Horse Care & Conformation

Advanced horse health, veterinary awareness, conformation assessment, nutritional science, and equine fitness. Critical knowledge for responsible instruction.

Status: Foundational | Duration: Self-paced

Module 3: Riding Skills & Technique

Advanced riding skills, seat development, aids and communication with the horse, and discipline-specific techniques. Practical horsemanship mastery.

Status: Intermediate | Duration: Practical assessment

Module 4: Instruction Methodology

Teaching principles, lesson planning, communication strategies, learning styles, class management, and assessment techniques. Professional instruction skills.

Status: Intermediate | Duration: Theory & practical

Module 5: Advanced Topics

Specialized riding disciplines, advanced training techniques, performance coaching, and specialty areas in equestrian sports. Professional expertise development.

Status: Advanced | Duration: Specialized track

Module 6: Professional Practice

Business management, liability and safety, insurance, professional standards, ethical conduct, and client relations. Business skills for professional instructors.

Status: Professional | Duration: Theory-based

Module 7: Master Practitioner

Integration of all modules, mentoring skills, advanced assessment, research-based practice, and leadership in equestrian education. Master-level qualification.

Status: Expert | Duration: Comprehensive

Specialization Modules

After Module 5, qualified instructors may pursue specialized certifications:

  • Module 5.1: Jumping & Eventing Specialization
  • Module 6.1: Coaching & Competition Preparation
  • Module 7.1: Training of Trainers (Advanced Mentorship)

Flexible Learning Pathways

SANIP offers flexible options to suit different learning styles and schedules:

Module-by-Module

Study and complete modules at your own pace, or take breaks between modules as needed.

Intensive Programs

Complete multiple modules in concentrated timeframes for accelerated progression.

Blended Learning

Combine theoretical learning with practical hands-on instruction and assessment.

Prior Learning

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) available - test out of modules you've mastered.
