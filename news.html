EQASA celebrates 50 years in 2026

EQASA's 2026 New Year message marks 50 years since Charlotte Stubbs established SANIP (South African National Instructors Plan) in 1976. The organisation highlights the continuity of management, the work of provincial exam teams and assessors, and its long-standing relationship with the International Group for Equestrian Qualifications.

EQASA assessed 481 candidates in five provinces during 2025 across Modules 1–7, including the return of Module 7 examinations to South Africa for the first time since 2017.

EQASA also reminds candidates that it is an Independent External Assessment Body, not a Training Provider. It supplies the syllabus and recommended reading list; candidates are responsible for their own study, with or without a training provider or qualified mentor.
