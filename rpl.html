Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)

Apply for EQASA certification credit based on your previous experience and qualifications.

What is RPL?

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) is a process that allows experienced equestrian professionals to obtain EQASA certification credit for knowledge and skills they've already acquired outside the standard SANIP training pathway. If you have substantial experience as an instructor, coach, or riding professional, you may be eligible for RPL credit towards one or more modules.

Who is Eligible?

🏇 Experienced Riding Instructors

Professional instructors with 5+ years of teaching experience may qualify for Modules 1-3 credit.

👥 University Graduates

Graduates in equine-related fields may be eligible for Module 2 (Horse Care) credit.

🎓 Previous Certification Holders

Holders of internationally recognized equestrian certifications may qualify for various module credits.

RPL Process

Step 1: Submit Application

Complete the RPL application form with details of your experience, qualifications, and which modules you're applying for. Include supporting documentation such as certificates, references, or employment history.

Step 2: Assessment Review

Our RPL assessors will review your application and supporting documents. This typically takes 2-4 weeks. We may request additional information or clarification.

Step 3: Interview/Practical Assessment

Qualified applicants will be invited for an interview and/or practical assessment to verify your knowledge and skills align with module requirements.

Step 4: Decision & Award

You'll receive notification of the RPL decision with details of any modules or partial credits awarded. Credits can be used towards progression through the EQASA qualification pathway.

Required Documentation

  • Completed RPL application form
  • Proof of identity (ID or passport)
  • Employment history or letters from employers
  • Copies of relevant qualifications and certifications
  • References from colleagues or students
  • Portfolio of work (if applicable)
  • Any published papers or resources you've created

RPL Fees

Single Module RPL

R 800 per module application

Multiple Modules

R 1,200 for 2 modules, R 1,500 for 3+

Full Qualification

R 2,000 to apply for full EQASA certification equivalency

Application Timeline

Submission to Review: 1-2 weeks

Assessment Review: 2-4 weeks

Interview/Practical (if required): 1-2 weeks

Decision Notification: 1 week

Total Estimated Time: 5-10 weeks
