Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)

Recognition of Prior Learning allows EQASA to recognise learning and competence gained through previous experience.

RPL Process of Assessment

A prospective candidate submits a CV to EQASA. Where necessary, the candidate may have an interview with the Managing Director to assess whether they are a suitable RPL candidate. For instructor/coach applications, the CV must include detailed information on competition disciplines, achievements, and instruction and achievement of pupils.

Candidates then download the RPL application form, review the relevant subjects in the EQASA syllabus and identify the modules for which they believe their experience may qualify them for credits.

The completed RPL application is returned to EQASA with payment of the fee in full. Where assessment is through a Portfolio of Evidence (POE), the candidate completes and submits the portfolio.

Application Process

  1. Submit a CV to EQASA and, if required, attend an interview with the Managing Director.
  2. Download and complete the RPL application form.
  3. Review the EQASA syllabus and identify the modules for which RPL is being requested.
  4. Return the application with payment of the fee in full.
  5. Where applicable, prepare and submit a Portfolio of Evidence (POE).
  6. The application is reviewed by the RPL panel and the Managing Director provides feedback. This takes approximately four to five weeks.
  7. If assessment is by challenge examination, the candidate receives notice of the examination/on-site assessment.
  8. The RPL panel assesses the POE and/or challenge examination and awards or declines credit.
  9. Results are sent out by the CEO.

Portfolio of Evidence

A portfolio provides documentation and evidence to verify the learning claimed. It is both an information folder and a process through which prior learning experiences can be translated into educational outcomes, documented and assessed for credit.

Most portfolios can be completed in approximately four weeks. An RPL candidate has six months after registering and paying the fee to submit the portfolio.

Assessment

Portfolios can be submitted throughout the year and will be assessed in the shortest possible time. EQASA assists RPL candidates through individual face-to-face appointments, telephone contact, email and postal services.

Cost

The RPL cost does not include the EQASA once-off registration fee, which should be completed in advance. The full RPL cost is provided on application and is approximately one-third of the price of the prescribed modules, plus the applicable assessment costs.
