Module 1: Foundations of Equestrian Training

Comprehensive introduction to equestrian fundamentals and horsemanship principles.

Module Overview

Module 1 is the foundational course in the EQASA qualification pathway. It introduces fundamental principles of horsemanship, including horse behavior, basic riding techniques, safety protocols, and the fundamentals of horse training. This module is required for all students and typically takes 4-6 weeks to complete.

Learning Objectives

By completing this module, you will be able to:

  • Understand horse psychology and behavior
  • Demonstrate safe riding and handling techniques
  • Apply basic grooming and tack maintenance
  • Understand horsemanship and ethical training principles
  • Identify basic health and safety concerns
  • Explain fundamental training methods

Course Content

Section 1: Horse Behavior & Psychology

  • Natural instincts and herd dynamics
  • Understanding horse communication
  • Behavioral problems and solutions

Section 2: Riding Fundamentals

  • Seat and position at walk, trot, and canter
  • Balance and coordination
  • Basic gaits and their characteristics

Section 3: Safety & Equipment

  • Protective equipment requirements
  • Tack fitting and maintenance
  • Common safety procedures

Section 4: Training Principles

  • Principles of positive reinforcement
  • Basic training exercises
  • Introduction to dressage basics

Assessment & Exams

Theory Exam

Format: 2-hour written exam with multiple choice, short answer, and essay questions

Content: All learning objectives and course content

Pass Mark: 60%

Practical Assessment

Format: 1-hour practical riding assessment

Content: Demonstrate correct seat, position, and basic riding techniques

Pass Mark: 60%
