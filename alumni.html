SANIP Alumni Network

Connect with EQASA certified professionals from across South Africa.

Who Are Our Alumni?

The SANIP alumni network includes over 500 EQASA certified riding instructors, coaches, and equestrian professionals. Our graduates work in private riding schools, racing stables, therapeutic programs, and equestrian centers throughout South Africa.

Why Join the Alumni Network?

🤝 Professional Networking

Connect with fellow EQASA certified professionals and build relationships within the equestrian community.

📚 Continuing Education

Access webinars, workshops, and resources for ongoing professional development.

💼 Career Opportunities

Job postings and professional opportunities shared exclusively with alumni members.

📰 Alumni Newsletter

Stay updated with industry news, member features, and events.

Recent Alumni Features

Instructor Spotlight: Maria Pieterse

Location: Stellenbosch, Western Cape | Certification: Level 3 Instructor

Maria specializes in teaching young riders and has coached several provincial champions. She runs a successful riding school focusing on horsemanship and safe riding practices.

Success Story: James van der Merwe

Location: Johannesburg, Gauteng | Certification: Level 4 Professional

After completing his EQASA certification, James launched his equestrian facility and now employs 5 instructors. He's also developed a therapeutic riding program for special needs riders.

Staying Connected

Once you complete your EQASA certification through SANIP, you're automatically part of our alumni network. You can:

  • Receive our bi-monthly alumni newsletter
  • Attend exclusive alumni events
  • Access our alumni directory
  • Participate in professional development opportunities
  • Share job opportunities with the community
