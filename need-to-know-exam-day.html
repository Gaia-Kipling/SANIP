What to Expect on Examination Day

4.1 Dress

Wear practical, comfortable everyday riding wear; show dress is not required. EQASA recommends a plain unbranded polo shirt, jodhpurs and short boots. Bring gloves for lungeing, a hard hat for work with horses and riding, and a crop. Do not wear spurs for teaching or riding unknown horses.

4.2 Arrival & Briefing

Arrive at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start. You will be briefed on the yard layout and assessment procedure, receive an examination number and receive the timetable for your sessions. The regional director conducts the briefing. EQASA cannot be responsible if you miss it.

4.3 Assessment Venue

Spectators who are not directly involved in assessments may not observe, record or photograph assessments. Please respect the assessment host and venue and do not loiter or litter.

4.4 Food & Refreshments

Bring your own lunch and/or refreshments. Pens and paper are provided for written components.

4.5 If You Have Difficulties

Speak to the assessment secretary or regional director if you encounter difficulties on the day.

4.6 Results

Assessors cannot confirm or deny success in a particular subject during or after the examination. The National Moderator collates and processes final results, which are posted on the website.

4.7 A Final Reminder

EQASA's aim is to establish competence and provide a positive assessment experience. Assessors understand the stress of examinations and are familiar with what candidates are expected to demonstrate.

By formalising and presenting your knowledge for certification, you are joining professionals working with horses and may be eligible to join the Council of Equine & Equestrian Professionals of South Africa (CEEPSA) and obtain a professional designation.
