Exam Day Requirements

Everything you need to know to prepare for your EQASA examination day.

What to Bring

  • Valid photo ID or passport
  • Exam confirmation email (print a copy)
  • Appropriate riding attire (boots, helmet, breeches, etc.)
  • ASTM or CE-certified riding helmet
  • Any prescribed medication (if needed)
  • Water bottle and snacks for breaks

Theory Exam Guidelines

Timing

Arrive 15 minutes before your exam. Late arrivals may forfeit entry.

Duration

Most theory exams are 2 hours. You cannot leave early, but you may finish early if you choose.

Materials Allowed

No books, notes, phones, or unauthorized materials. Calculators may be provided if needed.

Conduct Rules

Exam conduct standards apply. Cheating or misconduct will result in immediate disqualification.

Practical Exam Guidelines

Before Your Exam

Arrive at least 30 minutes early. Bring your horse/pony in good condition. Tack should be clean and well-maintained. Wear appropriate riding attire meeting competition standards.

During Your Exam

Follow all assessor instructions. Listen to feedback but do not argue with assessors. Demonstrate your best skills. The assessor may ask you to repeat movements or provide explanations.

Safety

Helmets must be worn at all times while mounted. Horses with unsafe behavior may be eliminated. Report any concerns to the assessor immediately.

Results Timeline

  • 1-2 weeks: Theory exam results sent to candidates
  • 2-3 weeks: Practical exam results sent to candidates
  • 2-4 weeks: Detailed feedback reports available online
  • 6-8 weeks: Certificates issued for passing candidates
