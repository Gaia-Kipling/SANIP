Recommended Reading

EQASA provides a recommended reading list rather than learning material. Candidates are responsible for their own self-study.

Recommended Reading

  • The BHS Complete Manual of Stable Management
  • The Threshold Guide to Lungeing – Book #36
  • EQASA Yard Management & Finance – Viv Woollon
  • Horse Conformation, Structure, Soundness & Performance – Equine Research Inc
  • A Guide to Animal Diseases in South Africa – Horses, Donkeys and Mules – Pamela Oberon
  • Horseman's Veterinary Encyclopedia – Equine Research Inc, 2005
  • The Principles of Riding – Official Handbook of the German National Equestrian Federation
  • The BHS Manual for Coaching & Teaching Riding – Islay Auty
  • The Manual of Horsemanship – Official Manual of the Pony Club

Animal Welfare & Horse Movement

For Animal Welfare Acts, candidates should research the Animals Protection Act 1962 (Act 71 of 1962) and the Performing Animals Protection Act 1935 (Act 24 of 1935).

For movement of horses and relevant travel documentation, the original EQASA reading list refers candidates to MyHorse and the South African Equine Veterinary Association resources on passports and movement of horses.

Welfare & Learning Resources

For Social License to Operate and welfare topics across all Modules, EQASA refers candidates to the FEI Ethics and Wellbeing Commission and World Horse Welfare.

For equine learning theory and how horses learn, EQASA recommends resources from Dr Andrew McLean and Equitation Science International.

Optional Extra Resource

FEI Campus provides free online courses.
