Recommended Reading

Recommended references and additional resources for EQASA candidates.

Recommended References

  • The BHS Complete Manual of Stable Management
  • The Threshold Guide to Lungeing – Book #36
  • EQASA Yard Management & Finance (Viv Woollon)
  • Horse Conformation, Structure, Soundness & Performance (Equine Research Inc)
  • A Guide to Animal Diseases in South Africa – Horses, Donkeys and Mules (Pamela Oberon)
  • Horseman’s Veterinary Encyclopedia (Equine Research Inc, 2005)
  • The Principles of Riding (Official Handbook of the German National Equestrian Federation)
  • The BHS Manual for Coaching & Teaching Riding (Islay Auty)
  • The Manual of Horsemanship (Official Manual of the Pony Club)

Welfare & Professional Resources

Candidates should research the South African legislation governing animal welfare, including the Animals Protection Act 1962 and the Performing Animals Protection Act 1935.

For equine welfare and learning resources, EQASA also recommends researching FEI welfare resources, World Horse Welfare, and Equitation Science International.

Optional Online Resource

FEI Campus provides free online courses and learning resources.
