Module 5.1 – International Level 1 Western Riding Coach

SANIP Certificate – International Level One Coach (Western Riding)

Henry Grimes Syllabus

Entrance Requirements

  • Grade 12 school-leavers certificate.
  • Successful completion of Modules 1, 2, 3 and 4, with no jumping requirement.
  • The minimum assessment age is 18 years.

Certification Information

  • The minimum pass rate for every subject is 70%.
  • Certification requires a recognised Level One First Aid Certificate and Grade 12 school-leavers certificate, or equivalent.

Subject Knowledge and Skills

  • Personal riding on a known horse and an unknown horse.
  • Individual Western riding lesson.
  • Group Western riding lesson.
  • Theory of Teaching 2.
  • Portfolio evidence, including 50 logged hours of personal riding and 70 logged hours of teaching.

Riding Level One

Candidates ride two horses: their own or a leased horse for reining and an unknown horse for the second assessment. They must demonstrate an established basic position in all three gaits, balance and coordination, transitions, turns on or about the forehand and hind leg, leg yielding, simple changes, speed and gait regulation, and the ability to discuss the horse’s way of going and basic training principles.

Teaching Level One

The candidate must demonstrate the ability to teach an individual and a group the basic principles of correct Western riding, including rider position, the horse’s way of going and sound teaching practice. Teaching must be appropriate to Rookie and non-pro level.

General Equestrian Knowledge 2

The written paper covers teaching-related Western riding knowledge, including the scales of training.
