Module 5.1: Dressage Specialization

Classical dressage training and advanced instruction in dressage discipline.

Specialization Overview

This specialization focuses on classical dressage principles, from basic training through advanced levels. Learn the biomechanics of dressage movement, training progression, and teaching techniques for dressage riders and horses.

Topics Covered

  • Classical dressage principles and history
  • Biomechanics of dressage movement
  • Training progression from basic to Grand Prix
  • Dressage riding techniques and position
  • Teaching dressage effectively
  • Competition preparation and judging

Requirements

Prerequisite: Module 5

Duration: 8-10 weeks

Fee: R 4,000
