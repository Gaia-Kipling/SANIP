Module 5 – International Level 1 Coach

SANIP Certificate – International Level One Coach

Professional Equestrian Coach (L1)

Entrance Requirements

  • Grade 12 (matric) school-leavers certificate or equivalent.
  • Successful completion of Modules 1, 2, 3 and 4.
  • The minimum assessment age is 18 years.

Certification Information

The minimum pass rate for all subjects is 70%. For certification, candidates must hold a recognised Level One First Aid Certificate and a Grade 12 school-leavers certificate or equivalent.

Subject Knowledge and Skills

  • 5.5.1 Personal Riding – flatwork and jumping.
  • 5.5.2 Individual Lesson on the Flat.
  • 5.5.3 Group Jumping Lesson.
  • 5.5.4 Theory of Teaching 2.
  • 5.5.5 Portfolios of Evidence – riding and teaching/lesson logs.

Assessment Focus

Personal riding includes flatwork and jumping at the level specified by the syllabus. Coaching assessments cover individual flat lessons and group jumping lessons, including assessment of horse and rider, identification and correction of faults, clear instruction, safety and appropriate training principles.

The Theory of Teaching assessment covers areas including competition rules, scales of training, safety, horse and rider suitability, problem solving, dressage arena layout and construction of jumps and gymnastic exercises.

Portfolios of Evidence

Candidates must maintain the prescribed riding and teaching records. The EQASA Required Documents Checklist should be used for the required evidence and hours.
