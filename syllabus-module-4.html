Module 4: Teaching and Instruction Methods

Master the art and science of teaching equestrian skills effectively to diverse learners.

Module Overview

Module 4 develops your ability to teach riding and equestrian knowledge effectively. Learn about different learning styles, lesson planning, communication techniques, and assessment methods. This module is essential for anyone aspiring to become a professional instructor. Duration: 4-6 weeks.

Learning Outcomes

  • Plan and deliver effective lessons for different skill levels
  • Understand learning styles and adapt teaching accordingly
  • Use clear communication and demonstration techniques
  • Assess student progress and provide constructive feedback
  • Manage group dynamics and individual student needs
  • Develop curriculum and training programs

Key Topics

Lesson Planning

Structured approach to designing progressive lessons with clear objectives and assessments.

Communication Skills

Effective verbal and non-verbal communication for clear instruction and feedback.

Assessment Methods

Formal and informal assessment techniques to evaluate student progress.

Group Management

Strategies for managing groups of different sizes and maintaining safe learning environments.

Module Details

Duration: 4-6 weeks
Study Hours: 50-60 hours
Prerequisites: Module 1 recommended
Module Fee: R 3,500
Exam Fee: R 500
