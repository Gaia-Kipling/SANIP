Module 4 – Assistant Instructor

SANIP Certificate – Assistant Instructor

Module 4: Assistant Riding Instructor

Requirements

  • Successful completion of Modules 1, 2 and 3.
  • The minimum age is 18 years.
  • A recognised Level One First Aid Certificate and SAPS Clearance Certificate are required for certification.
  • EQASA Riding Proficiency Tests 1 and 2 must be signed off by a CEEPSA-registered instructor before the assessment, unless the candidate has Pony Club C+ or higher.
  • Teaching and riding logs must be signed off by a CEEPSA-registered Level One instructor and loaded onto the candidate profile.
  • A police clearance certificate is required for instructors.

Assessment Information

The minimum pass rate for all subjects is 70%. Examiners may stop or postpone riding components if safety or horse welfare is compromised.

Lessons and riding assessments are generally 30 minutes. The Theory of Teaching assessment is a one-and-a-half-hour written paper.

Subject Knowledge and Skills

  • 5.4.1 Basic Personal Riding – flatwork and jumping, including an independent balanced seat and riding an unknown, experienced horse in all three paces.
  • 5.4.2 Group Lesson – safe and effective instruction of a group.
  • 5.4.3 Lunge Lesson – teaching through a lunge lesson.
  • 5.4.4 Theory of Teaching – lesson planning, teaching principles, risk management, instructor qualities, age groups, safe outrides, mounted games and pony camps.
  • 5.4.5 Portfolios of Evidence – personal riding and lessons/teaching logs using the prescribed formats and required hours.

Important

All candidates are expected to work within the syllabus requirements and maintain appropriate safety and horse-welfare standards throughout practical assessments.
