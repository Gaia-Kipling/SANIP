Modules 3-7 Overview

Advanced qualifications in riding, instruction, and professional equestrian practice.

Module 3: Riding Skills

Duration: 6-8 weeks | Fee: R 3,000 | Type: Practical focus

Advanced riding techniques including collection, extension, lateral movements, and jump training. Emphasis on technical proficiency across multiple disciplines.

Learn More

Module 4: Instruction Methods

Duration: 4-6 weeks | Fee: R 3,500 | Type: Theory + Practical

Teaching methodology, lesson planning, communication techniques, and student assessment. Prepares instructors to teach effectively across different learning styles.

Learn More

Module 5: Advanced Topics

Duration: 8-12 weeks | Fee: R 4,000 | Type: Specialty track

In-depth study of advanced disciplines including dressage, jumping, western, and endurance. Choose your specialization and become an expert in your chosen field.

Module 5 5.1 Specialization

Module 6: Professional Practice

Duration: 4-6 weeks | Fee: R 3,000 | Type: Theory + Business

Business management, professional liability, legal responsibilities, and business finance. Essential for instructors establishing their own practice or managing equestrian facilities.

Module 6 6.1 Specialization

Module 7: Master Practitioner

Duration: 10-14 weeks | Fee: R 4,500 | Type: Comprehensive

Integration of all previous modules with capstone project and master-level assessment. Culminates in EQASA Master Instructor certification.

Module 7 7.1 Specialization

Pathway Recommendations

Professional Instructor Path

Modules 1 → 2 → 4 → 6 → Complete business-focused instruction certification

Elite Rider Path

Modules 1 → 2 → 3 → 5 → Advanced riding specialization and technical excellence

Master Instructor Path

Modules 1-6 → 7 → Complete master certification for advanced practitioners
