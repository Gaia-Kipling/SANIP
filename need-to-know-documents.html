EQASA Required Documents: Checklist

Documents and logged records that candidates need to upload to their profile when required for an examination booking.

Important: Check the relevant syllabus for the exact requirements for your module. Documents must be uploaded to your profile at the time of exam booking.

Modules 1 & 2

  • Candidates aged 16 or 17: Parent/Guardian Letter of Consent

Module 3

  • Current Level One First Aid Certificate
  • Conformation Log – 3 hours
  • Lungeing Log – 3 hours

Module 4

  • Criminal Clearance Document
  • FEI Campus Safeguarding Certificate or another relevant certificate
  • EQASA Riding Proficiency Tests A & B
  • Teaching Log – 25 hours
  • Personal Riding Log – 25 hours

Module 5

  • Grade 12 or equivalent School Leaving Certificate
  • Teaching Log – 50 hours, including the additional hours required from Module 4 with an emphasis on competition riders
  • Riding Log – 70 hours, including evidence of competition riding
  • Criminal Clearance Check not older than 2 years
  • Current First Aid Certificate
  • FEI Campus Safeguarding course certificate or similar safeguarding certification

Module 6

  • Teaching Log – 75 hours teaching the nominated competition discipline(s), with evidence of pupil participation
  • Personal Riding Log – 75 hours personal competition riding in the nominated discipline(s), with evidence of participation
  • Current First Aid Certificate
  • Criminal Clearance Check not older than 2 years
  • FEI Campus Safeguarding course certificate or similar safeguarding certification

Module 7

  • Teaching Log – 200 hours of lessons taught in the nominated competition discipline(s), with evidence
  • Riding Log – 100 hours of personal riding in the nominated competition discipline(s), with evidence
  • Current First Aid Certificate
  • Criminal Clearance Check not older than 2 years
  • FEI Campus Safeguarding course certificate or similar safeguarding certification
