About Us

“EDUCATION BEFORE PARTICIPATION”

Since 1976, equestrian education in southern Africa has grown through a range of learning pathways and certification. The programme formerly operated under the SANEF National Instructors’ Plan; changes in the equine and equestrian industry and the wider educational environment led to the transformation of the NIP into EQASA.

EQASA’s vision is to address equine and equestrian educational needs for the benefit of all and to grow pride in certified knowledge. Alongside stand-alone subject-skills competency certifications, EQASA offers 12 levels of certification.

Certification Pathways

The instructor/coaching pathway progresses sequentially through the following SANIP certificates:

  • Stable Yard Skills – Stage 1 (Module 1)
  • Stable Yard Skills – Stage 2 (Module 2)
  • Horsemastership (Module 3)
  • Assistant Instructor (Module 4)
  • Instructor/Coach Level 1 (Module 5)
  • Instructor/Coach Level 2 (Module 6)
  • Instructor/Coach Level 3 (Module 7)

Stud Pathway

  • Stud Groom (Module 1)
  • Stud Head Groom (Module 2)
  • Apprentice Stud Yard Manager (Module 3)
  • Assistant Stud Yard Manager (Module 4)
  • Stud Yard Manager (Module 5)

International Recognition

Through EQASA’s membership of the International Group for Equestrian Qualifications (IGEQ), holders of EQASA Instructor/Coach Certificates at Levels 1, 2 or 3 are entitled to apply for International Equestrian Passports, which are recognised by the FEI.

Candidates from outside South Africa are welcomed.

Requirements

A recognised Level One First Aid Certificate is compulsory at Modules 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7. Candidates may take examinations before obtaining First Aid certification, but EQASA certification is only awarded once the candidate holds a current recognised certificate.

Practical experience is a prerequisite. Candidates are responsible for self-study; EQASA does not offer preparedness training for examinations. EQASA welcomes structured training initiatives but does not endorse, accredit or recommend a particular establishment. Candidates considering private training are encouraged to ensure that the training body is a member of the Council of Equine & Equestrian Professionals of South Africa (CEEPSA).

Specialisations

EQASA also offers certification pathways in Western Riding, Equestrian Tourism, Vaulting, Driving, Endurance, Saddle Seat, Polocrosse, Mounted Games, Tentpegging, Polo, Mounted Policing and Therapeutic instruction in association with SARDA.

Candidates for these codes are required to complete the Module 1, 2 and 3 components. Requirements from Module 4 onward for these specialisations are provided on request from EQASA.

Registration

All candidates are required to register on the SANIP website. The live site currently states a registration fee of R385 and notes that, because of applicant numbers, published examination dates in the Western Cape or Gauteng represent the start date of an examination week.
