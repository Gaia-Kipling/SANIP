Module 7 – International Level 3 Coach

SANIP Certificate – International Level Three Coach

Henry Grimes Syllabus

Entrance Requirements

  • Successful completion of Modules 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6.
  • The minimum assessment age is 22 years.
  • Evidence of at least 200 hours of lessons taught in the nominated competition discipline(s), submitted at least one month before examination.
  • Evidence of at least 100 hours of personal riding in each nominated discipline, submitted at least one month before examination.
  • Evidence of the candidate’s and pupils’ competition achievements.

Certification Information

  • Minimum pass rate for all subjects: 75%.
  • A current recognised Level One First Aid Certificate is required for certification.

General Equestrian Knowledge Level 3 – Oral

The oral examination is conducted as a discussion with one or more examiners and covers horsemanship, horsemastership and scenario-based subjects. Candidates are expected to provide professional and comprehensive responses.

Conformation & Unsoundness – Practical

Candidates need advanced technical knowledge and must demonstrate the ability to source, assess and select horses for competition purposes.

Riding Level 3 – Practical

The assessment includes riding known and unknown horses. Dressage candidates ride to Medium level and demonstrate advanced work such as half-pass and single flying changes. Show-jumping candidates jump a course at 1.2 m including a treble combination. Requirements for other competition disciplines are available from the managing director.

Practical Coaching

Candidates are assessed in one or more disciplines for which they seek Level Three accreditation. They must demonstrate an understanding of advanced training issues and teach the nominated discipline at higher competition level.

Communication and the Principles of Coaching – Written

The written assessment requires candidates to demonstrate their ability to design an applicable training schedule for a competition rider.
