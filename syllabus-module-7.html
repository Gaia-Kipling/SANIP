Module 7: Master Practitioner

Elite-level EQASA qualification for master instructors and advanced practitioners.

Module Overview

Module 7 is the culmination of the EQASA qualification pathway. This comprehensive module integrates all previous learning with advanced research, mentorship, and a capstone project. Only open to candidates who have completed Modules 1-6. Duration: 10-14 weeks.

Requirements

  • Completion of Modules 1-6
  • Minimum 5 years professional equestrian experience
  • Current professional liability insurance
  • References from recognized professionals

What's Included

Mentorship Program

Work with an experienced master instructor throughout the module.

Capstone Project

Develop and present a significant research or training project in your specialization.

Comprehensive Assessment

Multi-stage assessment including theory, practical, teaching demonstration, and capstone presentation.

Module Details

Duration:10-14 weeks
Study Hours:150+ hours
Prerequisites:Modules 1-6 completed
Module Fee:R 4,500
Exam Fee:R 750

Upon Completion

Successful completion of Module 7 results in an EQASA Master Instructor Certification, recognized throughout Southern Africa. You join an elite group of the most qualified equestrian professionals.
