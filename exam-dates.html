Important Booking Information

The closing date for bookings is shown against the relevant examination event. For Gauteng and Western Cape full examination weeks, bookings generally close four weeks before the examination date and late entries cannot be accepted.

Where a host does not achieve a viable number of candidates, EQASA reserves the right to amend dates. In the event of an overflow of applications, the same principle applies. Timetable details are sent to candidates before examinations.
