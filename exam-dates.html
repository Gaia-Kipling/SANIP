How to Register for Exams

Step 1: Complete Your Module

Finish studying and complete all assessments for the module you wish to examine.

Step 2: Register Online

Log in to your account and register for your preferred exam date and venue.

Step 3: Pay Exam Fee

Pay the examination fee. This is separate from module registration fees.

Step 4: Receive Confirmation

Receive exam confirmation email with venue details and what to bring.

Examination Fees

Examination Fee per Module: R 500

Practical Assessment Fee: R 800

Fees are non-refundable after registration deadline. Postponement available up to 14 days before exam.

What to Bring to Your Exam

  • Valid ID or passport
  • Exam confirmation email printout
  • Appropriate riding attire for practical exams
  • Helmet and riding boots meeting safety standards
  • Any required study materials (check your exam notice)
